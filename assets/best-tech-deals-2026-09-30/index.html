Sony WH-1000XM5

Buy now · Best Buy direct

Sony’s benchmark ANC over-ears at a rare $100 off.

What matters

At $298, the XM5 drops exactly $100 from Best Buy’s visible $398 reference. That price held for nine straight days before this check, which matters more than a one-hour flash badge: it suggests a stable, intentional promotion rather than a listing glitch. It is one generation behind Sony’s newest flagship, but that is precisely why this deal works. The mature model gives up novelty, not the core experience most buyers want.

Buy now · switch-bot.com

A stable 15% code discount on a biometric lock with no required subscription.

Code FNFN15 takes the Lock Vision Pro from $229.99 to $195.49, exactly 15% off.
