Editorial accountability

Correction policy

Accuracy matters more than saving face. We review specific correction requests, update the affected article when the evidence supports a change, and record material corrections below.

How to request a correction

Email gadgetauditinfo@gmail.com with the article title or URL, the exact passage you believe is wrong, the proposed correction, and a primary or authoritative source when one is available.

1 · ReviewThe Research Desk checks the claim against the article’s cited evidence and current primary sources.
2 · UpdateConfirmed errors are corrected promptly. Material edits receive a dated note in the article’s Recent updates box. PC Gamer’s dated changelogs, review-history widgets and GPU price-over-time graphs are the reference model: every material edit dated, review history searchable, and price-over-time graphs wherever prices are tracked.
3 · RecordChanges that alter a fact, conclusion, price context, safety point or recommendation enter the public log.

What enters the public log

We log factual corrections and material clarifications. Routine spelling, punctuation, formatting and accessibility fixes do not enter the log unless they changed the meaning of a passage.

What every log entry will show

  • The date of the correction.
  • The affected article and passage.
  • What was previously stated and what replaced it.
  • Why the correction was made.

We do not quietly delete a confirmed material error. If a full article must be withdrawn, the page will retain a clear withdrawal notice and the reason.
